# This is my English 101 course
## Schedule of Assignments
Schedule of Assignments
Most weeks will have a similar rhythm: discuss composition concepts from The Norton
Field Guide to Writing (NFG), analyze writing techniques in an essay from NFG,
practice formal grammar in English Essentials, complete in-class writing assignments,
and submit an essay on the weekend. Your instructor will clarify what to work on for
each day.
